# Northwind Travel Insurance — Plus Plan

This booklet sets out the cover provided under the **Plus** plan. Read it together with the
Common Terms booklet, which contains definitions, territorial scope and the complaints procedure.
Each clause has a reference (for example `[DLY-1]`). Decision letters will quote these references.

## [DLY-1] Flight delay benefit

If your outbound or return flight departs late by at least six (6) full hours compared with the
scheduled departure time shown on your booking, we will pay a fixed benefit of **$100 for each full
six (6) hours of delay**. The delay is measured from the scheduled departure time to the actual
departure time, both converted to the same time zone. Part-periods are not paid; for example, a
delay of 11 hours 50 minutes counts as one full period.

The maximum we will pay under this clause for any one trip is **$400**.

You must provide written confirmation from the airline of the scheduled and actual departure times
and of the reason for the delay.

## [DLY-2] Flight delay exclusion — announced strikes

We will not pay any flight delay benefit where the delay is caused by a strike or industrial action
that was publicly announced **more than 72 hours before** the scheduled departure time of your flight.
Strikes announced 72 hours or less before scheduled departure are covered under [DLY-1].

## [BAG-1] Lost, stolen or damaged baggage — per item

If your baggage is permanently lost, stolen or damaged while in the custody of an airline, we will
pay the **original purchase price of each item, less 10% for each full year of the item's age**,
measured from the purchase date to the date of loss. Depreciation will not exceed 50% of the
purchase price. Items less than one full year old are not depreciated.

The most we will pay for any one item, pair or set is **$500**.

You must provide proof of purchase (receipt, invoice or bank statement) for every item over $50.

## [BAG-2] Baggage — total limit

The most we will pay for all items under [BAG-1] in respect of any one trip is **$2,500**.
The per-item limit in [BAG-1] is applied to each item first, and the results are added together
before this total limit is applied.

## [BAG-3] Baggage — Property Irregularity Report required

You must report the loss or damage to the airline and obtain a **Property Irregularity Report (PIR)**
within **24 hours** of arriving at your destination airport. We will not pay a baggage claim without
a PIR. If you have not yet supplied one, we will ask you for it before making a decision.

## [BAG-4] Baggage — exclusions

We will not pay for:

1. cash, bank notes, coins, travellers' cheques or gift cards;
2. jewellery, watches or valuables left unattended in a public place;
3. any single item packed in **checked baggage** with an original purchase price **over $500**,
   unless it was declared to the airline at check-in on a valuables declaration;
4. perishable goods, or damage caused by wear and tear.

## [CXL-1] Trip cancellation

If you have to cancel your trip before departure because of one of the following covered reasons,
we will pay your **non-refundable** travel and accommodation costs that you paid or contracted to pay
before the reason arose, up to **$5,000** per trip:

1. you, a travelling companion or a close relative become seriously ill or are injured, and a
   doctor certifies that travel is not advisable;
2. the death of a close relative;
3. you are called for jury service or as a witness in court.

You must provide evidence of the reason (for example a doctor's certificate) and evidence from the
travel provider that the costs are non-refundable.

## [CXL-2] Trip cancellation — exclusions

We will not pay cancellation costs arising from:

1. you deciding not to travel, or changing your plans, for any reason not listed in [CXL-1];
2. a medical condition that existed when you bought the policy and that you did not declare to us;
3. costs that can be refunded, credited or recovered from any other source.

## [MED-1] Emergency medical expenses abroad

If you suffer an unexpected illness or injury during your trip outside your country of residence,
we will pay the reasonable and customary costs of emergency medical treatment, hospital
accommodation and medically necessary transport, up to **$250,000** per trip.

## [MED-2] Medical claims — supporting documents

For any medical claim over **$5,000**, you must provide a report from the treating doctor
describing the diagnosis, the treatment given and the dates of treatment, together with the
itemised hospital invoice. We will ask you for the report before making a decision.

## [LIA-1] Personal liability

We will pay up to $1,000,000 for your legal liability for accidental injury to another person or
accidental damage to their property during your trip. We do not cover liability arising from the
use of motor vehicles, aircraft or watercraft, or from your business activities.

## [GEN-1] Deductible

A deductible of **$50** applies to each claim and is taken off the amount payable after all limits
have been applied. The deductible does **not** apply to the fixed flight delay benefit in [DLY-1].

## [GEN-2] Time limit for claims

You must submit your claim within **30 days** of the end date of your trip. Claims submitted after
this period will not be accepted, unless you can show it was not reasonably possible to claim sooner.

## [GEN-3] Fraud

If any claim is fraudulent, or you or anyone acting for you makes a material misrepresentation or
supplies a false or previously used document, the claim will be void, no payment will be made,
and we may cancel your policy and report the matter to the authorities.
