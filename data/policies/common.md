# Northwind Travel Insurance — Common Terms

These terms apply to every plan (Basic, Plus and Premier). They do not by themselves give cover;
the cover is set out in your plan booklet.

## [DEF-1] Definitions

- **Trip**: a journey that starts and ends in your country of residence, booked before it begins,
  lasting no more than 60 days.
- **Close relative**: spouse or partner, parent, child, sibling, grandparent or grandchild.
- **Scheduled departure time**: the departure time shown on your most recent booking confirmation
  issued by the airline before the day of travel.
- **Checked baggage**: baggage handed to the airline at check-in and carried in the hold.
- **Unattended**: not in your full view, or left where you cannot stop it being taken.
- **Non-refundable**: an amount that the travel provider will not refund, credit or rebook for you.

## [TER-1] Territorial scope

Cover applies worldwide, excluding travel to countries where the government of your country of
residence advises against all travel at the time you depart. Emergency medical cover does not apply
in your country of residence.

## [PAY-1] How we pay claims

Approved claims are paid by bank transfer to the account held on your policy record, normally within
five working days of the decision. We pay in US dollars. Amounts invoiced in another currency are
converted at the exchange rate on the date the cost was incurred.

## [CMP-1] Complaints procedure

If you are unhappy with a decision, reply to your decision letter within 60 days explaining why.
A senior adjuster who was not involved in the original decision will review the claim and reply
within 15 working days. If you remain unhappy, you may refer the matter to the independent
insurance ombudsman free of charge.

## [PRV-1] Your personal data

We use your personal data to administer your policy and assess claims. We never ask for your full
payment card number or passport number in a claim form. If you include them, we remove them before
the claim is processed. We may share data with airlines, hospitals and fraud-prevention agencies
where necessary to assess a claim.

## [CAN-1] Cancelling your policy

You may cancel your policy within 14 days of purchase for a full refund, provided you have not
travelled or made a claim. After 14 days, no refund of premium is due.
